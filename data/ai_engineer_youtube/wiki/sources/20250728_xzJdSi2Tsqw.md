## Summary

James Lowe argues that AI products need a product-management mindset with enough AI expertise to resolve feasibility, data, evaluation, probabilistic behavior, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop questions. Case studies from the UK Government's Incubator for AI show three reusable product patterns: evaluate core AI capability before committing to a product surface, explore many AI-assisted feature variants with real users and then remove most of them, and pivot platform strategy quickly when commercial tools or standards such as MCP change the right interface.

## Notes

- Traditional PM still balances viability, feasibility, and desirability, but AI adds higher experimentation and failure risk, performance monitoring, probabilistic user experience, guardrails, human-in-the-loop design, and the question of whether the AI behavior is possible at all. 02:25-04:03
- Lowe treats "AI product manager" as a mindset more than a mandatory job title: someone on the team must grapple with product, engineering, data, evaluation, and probabilistic AI constraints together. 04:08-04:40
- In the Consult project, building directly around existing NLP techniques such as BERTopic led to inaccurate and inconsistent outputs that did not satisfy user needs or legal thresholds. 05:10-06:25
- The team reset around AI capability: collect real user data, generate synthetic data for evals, optimize the package, and test outputs with real users before building the final product surface. 06:27-07:43
- The resulting Themefinder package was described as comparable to human work while being much faster and cheaper, and the capability-first work identified where human input was valuable in the pipeline. 06:50-07:17
- For Minute, the team used AI coding assistants to try many features across user groups, then cut back to what worked because exploratory AI-built features carried less emotional attachment. 08:55-09:42
- User testing showed the many-template, agenda, AI edit, AI chat, speaker-name prediction, and transcript-citation surface was overwhelming; focusing on probation-service users led to a much simpler Justice Transcribe workflow. 09:50-11:47
- Redbox shifted from digitizing ministerial paperwork, to secure LLM chat for civil servants, to an interface for i.AI tools and data; thousands of Cabinet Office users validated the chat use case before another platform shift arrived. 12:23-14:47
- Microsoft Copilot Chat becoming free for enterprise Microsoft users and MCP standardizing tool/data access meant Redbox should not remain the assumed primary client; the team invested in MCP so tools and data could reach Redbox, Copilot Chat, Anthropic, ChatGPT, or other enterprise clients. 14:50-15:54
