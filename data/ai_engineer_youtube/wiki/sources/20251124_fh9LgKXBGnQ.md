## Summary

Ofer Mendelevitch frames enterprise deep research as web-style deep research applied to private company corpora: autonomous multi-step agents retrieve, reflect, synthesize, and cite evidence from internal systems for document-heavy workflows such as RFP responses, employee onboarding guides, and investment memos. The talk emphasizes that this workflow depends on multimodal ingestion, hybrid retrieval, metadata, reranking, hallucination mitigation, access controls, deployment flexibility, and observability because factual accuracy and private-data boundaries are central enterprise constraints.

## Notes

- Deep research is described as an AI agent conducting an in-depth multi-step investigation, browsing or searching, collecting results, synthesizing them, and generating a comprehensive cited report for a question. (02:19-02:46)
- Enterprise deep research keeps that pattern but points it at private data, with multi-agent execution, reflection, final synthesis, enterprise data queries, and corpus understanding for planning. (03:14-03:47)
- The platform requirements mentioned include multimodal ingestion for images and tables, hybrid retrieval, metadata, reranking, hallucination detection and correction, security, RBAC, bring-your-own-model, custom prompts, observability, monitoring, and SaaS, VPC, or on-prem deployment. (00:31-01:41)
- Example enterprise workflows include answering many RFP questions from enterprise data sets, generating on-demand onboarding guides from Jira, Notion, Google Drive, or SharePoint, and drafting investment memos in financial services. (03:49-05:02)
