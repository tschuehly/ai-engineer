# From Hype to Habit: How We're Building an AI-First SaaS Company-While Still Shipping the Roadmap

## Summary

Rossella Blatt Vital and Deepsha Menghani describe AI-first SaaS transformation as a cross-company operating shift across strategy, ways of working, and people. The source argues that teams should move beyond adding AI features to existing workflows, keep present roadmap delivery and future capability discovery running in parallel, make experimentation a planned ritual, evaluate process by outcomes, and scale AI fluency through enablement plus self-service platforms.

## Notes

- AI-first is described as a move from isolated AI-enhanced features to rethinking how the company plans, builds, and delivers value through an AI lens. (02:00-02:24)
- The talk frames AI transformation as multi-dimensional across strategy, ways of working, and people rather than as a binary switch or overnight rebuild. (02:59-04:00)
- Sprout Social's example contrasts feature-owned AI outputs with a unified natural-language experience that combines meals, activity, digestion, and supplements into broader customer value. (07:26-08:43)
- Fast-changing AI capability makes three-month roadmaps feel stale quickly, so the speakers describe shifting from deterministic roadmaps toward ambiguity, learning, and discovery shaping the path. (06:27-07:09)
- Ritualized discovery means planned time for experimentation, hackathons, learning forums, and MVPs meant to validate direction rather than only launch quickly. (08:59-10:12)
- Process should be assessed like a product: whether it creates direction, unblocks teams, and helps better decisions happen faster; otherwise it should be iterated or removed. (10:17-11:17)
- AI transformation is presented as cultural transformation: companies need T-shaped builders, organization-wide fluency, and self-service platforms so AI thinking is normal outside the core AI team. (12:27-16:43)
