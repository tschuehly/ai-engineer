## Summary

Dani Grant and Chelcie Taylor frame early VC fundraising for AI engineers as a sales and narrative process: pre-seed and seed investors can back founders before revenue or product, but the founder must explain why this team has a unique insight now, build investor relationships with specific outreach, and support scale claims with design-partner, ICP, monetization, and product-velocity evidence.

## Notes

- Pre-seed and seed fundraising can happen before revenue or product, but investors are then backing the founder, team, recruiting ability, and vision rather than current traction. 01:00-02:42
- Early investor relationships can start before a formal raise through advice-seeking, thesis responses, and conversations tied to a warm signal. 04:30-07:28
- Cold outreach should have a specific ask and enough context to compel a response; it should not try to carry the whole pitch in a long email. 08:02-09:49
- AI-engineer founders can over-index on technology; the pitch needs to explain why the founder has a unique insight about the market, product, go-to-market, or customer segment. 14:07-15:23
- Before revenue, customer interviews, design partners, ACV guesses, pricing hypotheses, and ICP testing plans can make the path from wedge to large business more credible. 18:21-25:00
