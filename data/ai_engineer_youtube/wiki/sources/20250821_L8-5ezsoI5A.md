## Summary

HF0's AI startup demo-day pitches surface several reusable AI engineering patterns: media products need retrieval over massive generated asset volumes, voice AI can become a developer-extensible hardware interface, action agents need reliability more than abstract intelligence, enterprise growth data can be converted from messy interaction records into structured graphs, and LLM inference marketplaces can reduce provider heterogeneity behind one routing API.

## Extracted Concepts

- [Design AI creative systems for generated-asset retrieval](../concepts/design-ai-creative-systems-for-generated-asset-retrieval.md) - Krea frames high-volume generated media as a retrieval and management problem, not only a generation problem.
- [Make voice AI devices developer-extensible](../concepts/make-voice-ai-devices-developer-extensible.md) - OpenHome frames smart speakers as open, customizable voice AI platforms rather than fixed command devices.
- [Optimize action agents for reliability before abstract intelligence](../concepts/optimize-action-agents-for-reliability-before-abstract-intelligence.md) - Featherless AI argues that business value comes from agents reliably completing ordinary actions.
- [Extract enterprise interaction data into structured graphs](../concepts/extract-enterprise-interaction-data-into-structured-graphs.md) - Upside describes using LLMs to turn messy sales and marketing records into a structured map for attribution.
- [Abstract LLM inference behind one routing API](../concepts/abstract-llm-inference-behind-one-routing-api.md) - OpenRouter describes a single API that handles model switching, provider edge cases, caching, tool calling, rankings, and observability.

## Notes

- Krea describes a creative suite generating about a million images per day for one studio and asks how teams will find personalized content at that scale. (00:49-02:12)
- OpenHome criticizes command-based smart speakers and proposes an AI-driven smart speaker platform where developers build custom voice applications, personalities, home automation tools, and hardware form factors. (03:20-06:04)
- Coframe frames websites as one-size-fits-all surfaces and pitches an AI growth team for each customer experience, making adaptive personalization a product surface rather than a static page. (06:33-07:23)
- Featherless AI argues that many AI projects fail because of reliability, not lack of intelligence, and contrasts benchmark-smart models with action agents that can reliably fill forms, book tickets, sort email, or process invoices. (07:31-10:15)
- Upside describes sales and marketing teams hoarding messy interaction data in Salesforce-like systems, then using LLMs to extract structured details and build graph, analytics, and graph-learning systems for attribution. (11:20-13:39)
- OpenAudio describes S1 as an instructible voice model where users can control not only what is said but how it is said, positioning prosody and emotion control as model behavior. (15:08-16:12)
- OpenRouter describes a model marketplace with one API, low switching cost between models, tool-calling and edge-case handling, caching, regional routing, privacy controls, observability, rankings, prompt categories, and plugin middleware that can call MCPs and transform model outputs. (18:45-22:03)
