## Summary

Will Brown frames RL environments as a practical abstraction for scaling AI research beyond large labs: an environment combines a harness, tasks, tools, and rewards, and can function as an eval, synthetic-data engine, SFT or distillation source, and RL training loop. The talk argues that product-specific agent harnesses can become model customization environments, while registries and managed infrastructure make these artifacts easier to share, run, inspect, and improve.
