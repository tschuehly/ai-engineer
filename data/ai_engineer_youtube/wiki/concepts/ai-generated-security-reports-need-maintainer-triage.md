# AI-Generated Security Reports Need Maintainer Triage

Summary: Agent-generated vulnerability reports can multiply security-report volume faster than maintainers can safely review or fix them. Open-source projects need triage capacity, skepticism toward dramatic reports, and care before merging AI-suggested fixes.

Use when:
- Handling a surge of security advisories, bug reports, or PRs from automated scanners and coding agents.
- Deciding whether to accept an AI-generated security fix under maintainer pressure.

Details:
- OpenClaw received 1,142 security advisories, including 99 labeled critical, at roughly 16.6 reports per day; the volume exceeded what a single maintainer could handle manually, 04:15-05:07, 09:48-10:21.
- Steinberger says reports that loudly claim criticality are often low-signal, and many reports appear to be generated by agents, 05:07-05:22, 14:47-15:12.
- Maintainers still need human review because they cannot fully trust an agent to determine whether a report is real or whether a proposed fix is safe, 14:47-15:12.
- When a report includes a fix, the fix is often poor; rushing such fixes under overload can break the product, 15:41-16:04.
- Company-backed help, such as full-time contributors working through reports and hardening the codebase, became a practical way to survive the report volume, 09:48-10:21.
