# Surface Existing Company Information Before Redesigning Processes

Summary: Agents can create immediate leverage by making scattered existing company information usable, rather than starting with a large process redesign or a new system of record.

Use when:
- A workflow is slowed by information already present in Slack, meetings, issue trackers, or notes but not where users need it.
- Prioritizing enterprise agents that improve coordination without changing the official process.

Details:
- Ubl argues that the most valuable near-term agent work can be surfacing information that already exists in the company but is not practically usable by the people or systems that need it. (10:36-10:52)
- Issue trackers are a representative case: the current state may exist in Slack messages or meeting recordings even when the tracker is stale. (10:52-11:12)
- Instead of asking managers to manually request update lists, an agent can pre-research and synthesize updates from the existing information substrate. (11:16-11:35)
