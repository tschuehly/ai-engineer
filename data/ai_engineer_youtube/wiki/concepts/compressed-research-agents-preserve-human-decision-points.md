# Compressed Research Agents Preserve Human Decision Points

Summary: A practical first agent pattern is to automate only the research phase between a business event and a human decision, reducing cycle time without changing the decision authority or risk profile.

Use when:
- Automating a repeated operations workflow where humans still own final judgment.
- Looking for low-risk agent deployments before redesigning the full business process.

Details:
- Ubl describes many business processes as a business event, a research phase, and a human decision; the agent can perform the research automatically while the surrounding process stays the same. (08:37-09:10)
- This pattern can compress a 30-minute research task into a 5-minute review path at high volume without increasing the process's risk profile or requiring organizational process change. (09:10-09:27)
- Vercel examples include a sales-intake agent that researches companies and routes leads, and an abuse-report agent that investigates a reported website while a professional still makes the final decision. (09:27-10:25)
