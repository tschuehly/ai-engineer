# Use AI leapfrogging to find conservative-industry opportunities

Summary: Conservative or low-tech industries can adopt AI quickly when the product solves a concrete workflow pain that previous software did not handle well. Slow historical software adoption does not automatically mean slow AI adoption.

Use when:
- Looking for vertical AI startup opportunities beyond developer tools.
- Challenging the assumption that only already-technical industries will adopt AI products quickly.

Details:
- Guo calls this the AI leapfrog effect: beyond coding, some conservative low-tech industries appear to adopt AI fastest when the product is tied to real customer problems. 16:11-16:32
- Examples in the talk include Sierra resolving a large share of customer-service queries, Harvey becoming essential for legal competitiveness, and Open Evidence reaching clinicians with medical-research search at the point of decision-making. 16:32-17:24
- The opportunity is not generic chat; the cited companies know their customer, fit the workflow, and solve a specific problem that creates visible value. 17:16-17:24
