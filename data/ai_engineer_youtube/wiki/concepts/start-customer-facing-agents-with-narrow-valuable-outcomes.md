# Start Customer-Facing Agents With Narrow Valuable Outcomes

Summary: Customer-facing agent strategy should start with a concrete customer or business problem, even if the first workflow is narrow. A small, valuable action creates a safer learning loop than applying AI broadly and hoping strategy emerges.

Use when:
- Planning the first production surface for a support, care, or retail agent.
- Challenging vague "apply AI" strategy without a customer outcome.

Details:
- Bavor says successful customers do not let perfect block adoption, but they pair risk tolerance with real customer and business problems rather than generic AI experimentation. (06:34-07:34)
- A valid starting point can be as narrow as processing a single return, such as drop-shipping replacement shoes and providing a shipping label. (07:34-08:03)
- The source warns against shoehorning old team structures into the AI era; successful customers rearchitect customer service teams around supporting and improving the AI agent. (08:09-08:29)
- Post-launch operations can include people reviewing hundreds of conversations per day and coaching the agent on better decisions, empathy, wording, and judgment. (08:31-08:56)
