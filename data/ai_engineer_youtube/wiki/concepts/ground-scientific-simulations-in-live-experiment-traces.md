# Ground Scientific Simulations in Live Experiment Traces

Summary: Real experiment traces can turn scientific-agent simulation from abstract modeling into a calibrated search loop. Cameras, sensors, voice notes, sample metadata, and measured outcomes can inform simulations that propose better conditions for the next physical experiment.

Use when:
- Evaluating whether a scientific agent should use physical observations to improve simulations.
- Designing lab workflows that alternate between live experiments, analysis, simulation, and condition adjustment.

Details:
- The crystal-growth workflow recorded microscope video and CSV sensor values, then plotted growth rate, temperature, humidity, and sample-condition differences for later analysis. (12:16-13:45)
- The observed data changed the experimenter's understanding: crystal formation appeared as bursts after critical saturation rather than smooth gradual growth. (13:55-14:17)
- Future co-scientist loops can use camera, sensor, and voice traces to create simulations of processes such as crystal growth or bacterial colony growth. (17:16-17:46)
- The intended loop is to run simulations with recorded lab conditions, identify better experiment conditions, and then recreate those conditions in the real lab. (17:46-17:56)
