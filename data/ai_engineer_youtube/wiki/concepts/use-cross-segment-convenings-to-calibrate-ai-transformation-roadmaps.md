# Use Cross-Segment Convenings to Calibrate AI Transformation Roadmaps

Summary: AI transformation leaders can use cross-segment practitioner convenings to calibrate their roadmap against frontier labs, startups, academic work, consultancies, and large-company deployment examples.

Use when:
- Designing leadership learning loops for enterprise AI adoption.
- Deciding whether an AI roadmap is being shaped by too narrow a peer group or vendor narrative.

Details:
- Lieberman frames his move into AI around staying close to the frontier while helping mid-market and enterprise companies learn how to use AI in their businesses. (01:19-01:47)
- The AIE LEAD program is described as both retrospective industry synthesis and a tactical view of where companies of different ages and sizes are headed. (01:58-02:11)
- The speaker mix matters as a learning design choice: labs, AI startups, academics, management consultants, and Fortune 50 brands expose leaders to different constraints and adoption evidence. (02:11-02:21)
