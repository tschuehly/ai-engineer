# Enterprise Deep Research Runs Multi-Step Synthesis Over Private Corpora

Summary: Enterprise deep research adapts public-web deep research loops to private company data. The agent searches internal corpora, runs multi-step or parallel investigation, reflects on findings, and synthesizes cited reports for document-heavy enterprise questions.

Use when:
- Building a private research agent for RFPs, onboarding, due diligence, investment memos, or other document-centric workflows.
- Deciding whether a workflow needs a multi-step research loop instead of a single RAG answer.

Details:
- Mendelevitch defines deep research as an AI agent conducting an in-depth multi-step investigation, usually by browsing or searching, collecting results, synthesizing them, and producing a comprehensive report with citations. (02:19-02:46)
- Enterprise deep research keeps the same process but directs it at private enterprise data, using multi-agent execution, reflection, synthesis, and corpus understanding to plan from the organization's data. (03:14-03:47)
- RFP response is a fit because the agent can search enterprise data sets, find the right documents, and answer many structured questions that would be difficult to gather manually. (03:49-04:17)
- Onboarding guides are another fit when current documentation is scattered across Jira, Notion, Google Drive, SharePoint, or similar systems and static guides have gone stale. (04:17-04:47)
