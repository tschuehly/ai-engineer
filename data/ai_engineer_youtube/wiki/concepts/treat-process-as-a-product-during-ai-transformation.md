# Treat Process as a Product During AI Transformation

Summary: AI transformation processes should be evaluated by whether they create clarity, unblock teams, and speed better decisions; otherwise they become drag and should be iterated or removed.

Use when:
- Adding governance, planning, or operating rituals to an AI transformation program.
- Diagnosing why an AI-first operating model is slowing teams instead of helping them adapt.

Details:
- The source says older processes were designed for predictable linear roadmaps and deterministic risk, while AI roadmaps shift as capabilities evolve monthly. (10:17-10:37)
- Adding too many new processes at once can backfire by creating overhead and slowing the organization. (10:37-10:53)
- Treating process as a product means asking whether it creates direction, unblocks teams, and helps better decisions happen faster; if not, it should be iterated or removed. (10:53-11:17)
- The same section frames smart velocity as speed paired with clarity, momentum, and adaptability rather than shipping for the sake of shipping. (11:18-12:08)
