# Rank Architecture Recommendations by Business Impact

Summary: Architecture copilots should produce explainable, traceable recommendations ranked by business impact rather than generic best-practice advice. The ranking needs to account for cost, performance, risk, time to value, existing investments, and strategic goals.

Use when:
- Evaluating whether an AI architecture assistant is useful for CTO, architecture, or platform planning.
- Turning technical modernization options into roadmap decisions.

Details:
- The speakers frame architecture as the place where ROI is won or lost because the wrong direction can make high coding output create poor results, redo, and technical debt. (01:23-01:48)
- Leaders need to know what to prioritize and how to defend architecture decisions with data tied to ROI and spend management. (03:40-04:16)
- The core recommendation question is what to do next given constraints, existing investment, and strategic goals, not which project has the loudest advocate. (06:36-07:04)
- Recommendations should be explainable and traceable: why the recommendation is valid, where it came from, expected impact, measurable outcomes, and how each initiative maps to business objectives and best practices. (08:00-08:31)
- The source distinguishes straightforward best-practice moves from higher-value architecture changes that understand the overall system and improve reusability, cost, or performance across multiple applications. (16:30-17:14)
