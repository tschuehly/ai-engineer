# AI Agents Still Need Human Taste for Interaction Quality

Summary: Current coding agents can generate functional UI mechanics but may miss timing, feel, and interaction quality. Human review remains important for product surfaces where animation, latency perception, and emotional response define quality.

Use when:
- Reviewing agent-generated UI or animation work.
- Deciding which aspects of front-end quality require human design judgment.

Details:
- Artman argues current agents lack taste because they do not experience the product as a human user; they can inspect screenshots or DOM state but do not feel time, frustration, or interaction quality. (20:08-21:18)
- The transcript contrasts mechanically correct agent-generated animations with human-tuned animations that feel natural because easing, speed, and timing are adjusted for the interaction. (21:21-22:18)
- Linear's own interaction-quality example uses instant hover highlight plus a short fade-out to make the application feel both fast and smooth. (12:49-13:15)
