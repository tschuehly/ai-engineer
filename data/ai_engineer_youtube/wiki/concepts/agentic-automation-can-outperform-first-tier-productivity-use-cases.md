# Agentic automation can outperform first-tier productivity use cases

Summary: Early enterprise AI value often starts with time savings, content output, and quality improvement, but use cases that mention automation or agents can represent a higher-impact layer. The source treats this as an emerging signal from self-reported ROI data rather than a settled benchmark.

Use when:
- Prioritizing between simple productivity copilots and deeper workflow automation.
- Looking for enterprise AI use cases that may produce impact beyond individual time savings.

Details:
- Time savings was the largest reported category, at roughly 35% of use cases, and can still be meaningful when five to ten saved hours per week compound into several reclaimed work weeks per year. (10:39-11:36)
- The talk distinguishes first-tier gains such as more content, better content quality, and reclaimed time from automation and agentic use cases that appear later in adoption. (15:56-16:17)
- Use cases that mention automation or agents were described as outperforming the average in self-reported ROI, suggesting that deeper workflow delegation may be a next layer after basic productivity wins. (16:14-16:40)
- Risk-reduction use cases were uncommon as the primary benefit, but the source reports they were the most likely to claim transformational ROI, especially in high-volume back-office, compliance, and risk functions. (14:16-15:25)
