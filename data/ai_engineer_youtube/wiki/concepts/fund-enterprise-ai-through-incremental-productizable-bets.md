# Fund Enterprise AI Through Incremental Productizable Bets

Summary: Risk-averse enterprises can fund uncertain AI programs by breaking research into short stages that each produce a usable deliverable, measurable business value, and a decision point for stopping or continuing.

Use when:
- Planning AI transformation work where a large upfront moonshot would be hard to approve.
- Turning exploratory agent research into business-facing milestones that reduce sunk-cost pressure.

Details:
- Northwestern Mutual framed GenBI around stability and "generational responsibility," so leadership needed visible risk control before investing in an uncertain analytics agent. (02:26-04:48)
- The roadmap uses gradual stages that give leadership visibility into what is being funded, what deliverable comes back, and whether to continue, stop, or productize a stage. (10:05-10:44)
- Each six-week sprint is expected to return a tangible deliverable for the business, and each stage feeds learning into the next stage rather than becoming a disconnected demo. (13:39-14:09)
- The staged model reduces sunk-cost bias because a later vendor option or failed phase can stop the program while preserving internal benchmarks and business value already produced. (14:11-15:16)
