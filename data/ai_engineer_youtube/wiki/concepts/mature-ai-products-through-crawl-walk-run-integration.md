# Mature AI Products Through Crawl-Walk-Run Integration

Summary: AI product maturity can progress from embedded acceleration, to contextual personalized assistance, to interoperable agentic product experiences. Each stage should build reusable capability into the product suite rather than discard earlier work or bolt on a separate AI surface.

Use when:
- Planning how a mature product should introduce AI without immediately redesigning the whole application.
- Choosing when an AI feature requires new UI, UX, or application architecture.

Details:
- In the crawl phase, teams embed AI into existing workflows and backend functionality with little new product surface, such as semantic search over prior support questions or generated knowledge-base content. 07:40-08:50, 11:00-11:55
- Workday's crawl example used AI to turn policy documents into employee FAQs and manager talking points, then supported translation into 34 languages with version management for enterprise content teams. 11:18-12:42
- In the walk phase, teams add contextual and personalized AI surfaces while preserving the core app architecture, such as an assistant that sees the current Workday page and offers context-aware suggestions while respecting sensitive data boundaries. 08:50-09:20, 13:31-14:59
- In the run phase, teams need to rethink UI, UX, and app architecture because agentic capability operates across the product suite, such as autonomous support workflows or Workday assistant behavior that listens to policy changes, notifies users, and suggests actions. 09:20-10:16, 14:59-15:52
- The stages are cumulative: even crawl-stage features should be embedded in the product experience, and later stages extend earlier capability rather than replacing it with a disconnected AI product. 09:58-10:16
