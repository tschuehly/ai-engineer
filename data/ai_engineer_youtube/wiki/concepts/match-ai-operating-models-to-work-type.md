# Match AI Operating Models to Work Type

Summary: AI-assisted software teams should choose the human-agent operating model based on the work's context load, output determinism, and need for iteration. A legacy modernization task can fit a factory-style agent flow, while new feature development may need co-creative loops that generate options and shorten feedback cycles.

Use when:
- Deciding whether an AI coding workflow should be plan-heavy, review-heavy, factory-like, or exploratory.
- Reorganizing Agile ceremonies around human-agent collaboration instead of applying one process to every engineering function.

Details:
- Rewiring the product development lifecycle is not one-size-fits-all; different engineering functions across the product lifecycle may need different operating models based on how humans and agents collaborate. (07:36-07:52)
- Legacy modernization is framed as high-context work over potentially the whole codebase, but with well-defined outputs, making it a candidate for an agent factory where humans provide initial specs and final review. (07:54-08:15)
- Greenfield and brownfield feature work may benefit from nondeterministic variation, with agents acting as co-creators that offer more options and support faster feedback loops. (08:15-08:36)
- Work allocation becomes a new management problem because AI impact is uneven across tasks and people have uneven experience with AI tools. (05:09-05:54)
