# Treat Investor Outreach Like Specific Go-To-Market

Summary: Investor outreach works better when it is relationship-led, specific, and response-oriented, mirroring good go-to-market practice rather than trying to close the whole round in one message.

Use when:
- Designing cold or warm outreach for a founder fundraising process.
- Reviewing whether an investor email has a concrete reason to reply.

Details:
- The speakers recommend starting investor relationships before a formal raise: asking for advice and sharing early work can make the later fundraise less abrupt. 04:30-04:59
- Cold outreach can work when it is prompted by a warm signal, such as responding to an investor's current thesis, post, or event interaction. 06:18-07:28
- Effective messages make a specific ask, such as feedback on go-to-market or pointers to relevant investors, so the recipient knows what a short conversation would cover. 08:02-09:21
- The job of the email is to compel a response, not carry the whole pitch; long, complete pitch emails can reduce the chance of a meeting. 09:21-09:49
- Several examples worked because they created a shared baseline of thinking: they referenced the investor's article or thesis and connected it to the founder's current work. 09:51-12:47
