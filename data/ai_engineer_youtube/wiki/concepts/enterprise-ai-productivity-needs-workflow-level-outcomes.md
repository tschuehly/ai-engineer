# Enterprise AI Productivity Needs Workflow-Level Outcomes

Summary: Enterprise GenAI value should be measured at the workflow or engagement level, not only by whether individual workers feel faster. In knowledge work, useful AI compresses data-understanding and preparation phases so experts can spend more time on analysis, decisions, and client-facing outcomes.

Use when:
- Evaluating whether an internal AI platform is producing business impact rather than adoption activity.
- Designing knowledge-work automation around whole engagement phases instead of isolated prompt tasks.

Details:
- Professional-services engagements often start with large upfront data-room work: ingesting PDFs, databases, spreadsheets, and other artifacts, normalizing them, categorizing them, and placing them into a framework for analysis. (03:38-04:20)
- The source frames the client-valued output as the recommendation, deliverable, or business-problem solution; AI work should therefore be judged by whether it accelerates that path, not by demo novelty. (04:33-04:48)
- AlixPartners reports seeing AI compress the initial understanding phase, sometimes reducing the human effort needed to get up to speed on a data room from a large portion of the engagement to a smaller share. (04:48-05:07)
- The talk explicitly distinguishes employee productivity from enterprise productivity after citing the paradox that many firms invest in AI while broad productivity gains remain hard to observe. (06:36-07:24)
- Full-corpus review changes the workflow: instead of prioritizing only the highest-value 20% of documents because humans are time- or cost-limited, teams can inspect all contracts or customer records and free experts for higher-value work. (05:10-06:28)
