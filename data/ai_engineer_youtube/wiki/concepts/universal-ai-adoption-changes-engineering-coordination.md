# Universal AI Adoption Changes Engineering Coordination

Summary: AI-native engineering practices become qualitatively different when every engineer uses agents. Partial adoption can force the organization to keep traditional coordination paths, while universal adoption lets teams assume delegated, parallel, agent-readable workflows.

Use when:
- Evaluating why a team has not seen organizational leverage from scattered coding-agent use.
- Designing an AI-native engineering operating model.

Details:
- The source argues there is a "10x difference" between 90% and 100% AI adoption because even a 10% traditional cohort pulls the organization back toward editor-centric coordination. (02:11-02:50)
- Every is presented as a small-company lab: four software products are run by fifteen people, and the products are described as production businesses rather than toy demos. (03:10-03:41)
- The company claims 99% of its code is written by AI agents such as Claude Code, Codex, and Droid, with no one primarily handwriting code. (03:42-04:04)
- A single developer can own a complex production app when agent delegation, parallel work, and shared agent context are assumed as normal workflow primitives. (04:05-06:49)
