# Design AI Creative Systems For Generated-Asset Retrieval

Summary: High-volume AI creative tools create a retrieval and asset-management problem alongside the generation problem. When teams produce personalized media at studio scale, they need ways to find, organize, and reason over generated outputs.

Use when:
- Designing creative AI products that may generate many variants per campaign, studio, region, or user segment.
- Explaining why generative-media infrastructure needs indexing, search, and provenance around outputs, not only faster generation.

Details:
- Krea describes one studio generating about a million images per day, then asks how users can find the relevant generated content afterward. (00:49-01:08)
- The pitch connects the same issue to highly localized content, such as personalized media for towns in India, where the retrieval problem grows with personalization scale. (01:56-02:12)
- A two-day path from signup to payment to broadcast ad use suggests creative AI products need operational readiness for generated assets to leave experimentation and enter production media workflows quickly. (02:15-02:30)
