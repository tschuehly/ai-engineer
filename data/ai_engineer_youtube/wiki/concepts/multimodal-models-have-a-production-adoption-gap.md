# Multimodal Models Have a Production Adoption Gap

Summary: Text LLMs are much more widely used in workplace production than image, video, and audio models. Multimodal capability should therefore be planned as an adoption and workflow problem, not only as a model availability problem.

Use when:
- Estimating whether multimodal features are ready for broad production rollout.
- Prioritizing audio, image, or video investments against existing text AI adoption.

Details:
- The survey found that image, video, and audio usage at work lagged text usage by significant margins. (06:25-06:45)
- The gap persisted even when including respondents who had those modalities in production but had not gained as much traction. (06:48-06:56)
- Among respondents not currently using audio, 37% planned to adopt it, making audio the highest stated adoption-intent modality among non-users in this talk. (07:24-07:40)
- The source supports a cautious planning stance: multimodal products need deployment, UX, evaluation, and adoption work beyond access to capable models.
