# Ritualized Discovery Keeps AI Roadmaps Adaptive

Summary: Fast-moving AI roadmaps need planned discovery rituals so learning, experimentation, and failed bets continuously reshape direction instead of remaining isolated spikes.

Use when:
- Designing planning cycles for AI products whose feasible surface changes quickly.
- Preventing AI innovation from becoming ad hoc demos that never affect strategy.

Details:
- The source says AI capability changes can make a three-month roadmap feel stale in weeks, pushing teams away from deterministic roadmaps toward ambiguity, learning, and discovery as planning inputs. (06:27-07:09)
- Fragmented discovery, isolated spikes, and one-off ideas rarely become sustained strategic drivers when the AI landscape and stakes move quickly. (08:59-09:30)
- Ritualized discovery means building time into planning cycles for experimentation, hackathons, learning forums, and visible company-wide strategy inputs. (09:30-09:49)
- MVPs should be used for learning and direction validation, not only rapid launch; failed bets can clarify ambiguity when they are treated as expected discovery evidence. (09:49-10:12)
