# Evaluate Agents By Human-Agent Interaction Outcomes

Summary: Agent evaluation should include whether human-agent interaction improves human agency, creativity, productivity, strategic thinking, or flow. Model capability and engagement metrics are insufficient when the goal is augmentation rather than merely giving the model more autonomy.

Use when:
- Defining success metrics for general-purpose or computer-use agents.
- Challenging dashboards that optimize model scores, time spent, or raw automation without measuring human benefit.

Details:
- Perszyk contrasts a standard AGI vision of smarter, more agentic AI with a useful-general-intelligence vision where AI makes humans smarter and increases human agency. (06:45-07:25)
- Automation can augment humans by freeing attention, but it can also reduce agency through distraction, echo chambers, or autocomplete that shuts down thinking; precise control and tailoring are therefore part of the product goal. (06:00-07:00)
- The talk argues that general intelligence is social, distributed, and interactional, so teams should measure and optimize the interactions humans have with agents. (15:02-15:17)
- Suggested human-centered metrics include creativity, productivity, strategic thinking, and states of flow rather than only model capabilities or time spent on platform. (15:17-15:29)
