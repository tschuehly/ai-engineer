# Anchor AI Products on One Reliable User Outcome

Summary: AI product purpose should be defined as the single outcome the product must deliver reliably for its users. That outcome is the filter for whether a model capability is useful product work or distracting baggage.

Use when:
- Turning a model demo into a product direction.
- Deciding whether a new AI feature strengthens or dilutes the user's core job.

Details:
- The talk frames taste as outcome clarity rather than aesthetics: ask what single outcome the product must deliver every time for every user. 10:35-11:14
- Purpose acts as the north star for judging whether a feature is useful or just baggage, especially when AI capabilities make many demos easy to ship. 11:02-11:18
- In NotebookLM, the clear use case was putting many documents into one tool and interacting with them, rather than adding AI as a bolt-on to an existing document surface. 09:40-10:29
