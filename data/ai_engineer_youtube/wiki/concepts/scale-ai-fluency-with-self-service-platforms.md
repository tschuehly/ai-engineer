# Scale AI Fluency With Self-Service Platforms

Summary: AI-first companies need organization-wide fluency plus self-service platforms so teams can explore, prototype, and ship AI-powered work without every effort depending on a central AI team.

Use when:
- Planning AI enablement for product, engineering, design, marketing, support, or operations teams.
- Deciding whether an internal AI platform should reduce dependence on specialist AI teams.

Details:
- The source frames AI-first transformation as cultural transformation, requiring talent and fluency changes across the whole company rather than only the AI team. (12:27-13:07)
- Sprout Social's talent model emphasizes T-shaped builders with deep expertise who can also prototype quickly, collaborate across silos, navigate ambiguity, and build end-to-end systems. (13:11-15:07)
- Company-wide AI fluency is needed because marketers, designers, PMs, and care agents all touch AI even if they are not training models or building agents. (15:08-15:59)
- Enablement mechanisms include newsletters, podcasts, cross-functional AI show-and-tells, flexible tool use, and self-service platforms that let product and engineering teams prototype and ship AI-powered features with less deep involvement from the AI team. (16:01-16:43)
