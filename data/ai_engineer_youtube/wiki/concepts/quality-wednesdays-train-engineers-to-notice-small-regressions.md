# Quality Wednesdays Train Engineers to Notice Small Regressions

Summary: A recurring quality ritual can train engineers to notice tiny interaction, performance, and polish defects that ordinary feature work misses. The value is both the fixes themselves and the durable attention it creates.

Use when:
- Designing team routines that maintain UI and product quality under high implementation speed.
- Looking for a workflow that turns product taste from implicit judgment into repeated practice.

Details:
- Linear's Quality Wednesdays have engineers present one quality fix each, ranging from one-pixel visual adjustments to backend efficiency improvements. (12:00-12:37)
- The practice started after a focused team review of a tiny UI surface found roughly 35 problems that were invisible until engineers deliberately looked for them. (13:49-14:25)
- Linear reports fixing roughly 2,500-3,000 small details through this practice, and the meeting requirement made engineers watch for quality regressions during unrelated feature work. (14:48-16:04)
