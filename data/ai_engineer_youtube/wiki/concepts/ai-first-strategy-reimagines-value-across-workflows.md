# AI-First Strategy Reimagines Value Across Workflows

Summary: AI-first strategy is not adding intelligence to existing feature slots; it is rethinking what customer value becomes possible when AI can unify data, workflows, and roles.

Use when:
- Distinguishing AI-enhanced features from AI-first product strategy.
- Evaluating whether a roadmap is only sprinkling model calls into existing experiences.

Details:
- The source defines AI-first as evolving from AI features sprinkled into a product to rethinking how the company plans, builds, delivers value, and places AI at the center of strategy. (02:00-02:24)
- The strategy shift changes the question from where to add intelligence to an existing experience toward what new experiences were not possible before. (04:22-05:04)
- A health-tracking app example shows the difference: separate teams can produce narrow meal, activity, digestion, or supplement answers, while an AI-first experience combines those signals into one natural-language response with broader value. (07:26-08:43)
- The source also warns that future-oriented reimagination still has to run beside current customer delivery; over-indexing on present roadmap work risks missing the AI shift, while focusing only on the future can disappoint current customers and starve innovation. (05:18-06:26)
