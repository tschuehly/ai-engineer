# Agents Expand the Economically Viable Software Surface

Summary: Agents make more automation worth building by reducing the cost of encoding business knowledge and handling cases that were too expensive to hardcode as traditional software.

Use when:
- Estimating whether an agent should replace a manual workflow that was previously not worth software investment.
- Explaining why cheaper AI-assisted software creation can increase, not only reduce, software demand.

Details:
- Ubl describes "all software that should exist" as larger than what teams could economically build when every rule, branch, and business fact had to be discovered and hardcoded. (04:02-04:58)
- Agents can fill more of that useful-software surface because they make previously uneconomic automation viable with less implementation effort. (04:58-05:20)
- He frames the market as an elasticity experiment: as software gets cheaper to make, companies may choose to make more of it, and demand for software engineers can rise until the market reaches a later S-curve. (06:06-06:44)
