# Integrate AI Strategy Into Core Product Strategy

Summary: AI strategy should not run as a separate sidecar to product strategy. When AI initiatives are isolated from core roadmap ownership, teams tend to ship visible but undifferentiated AI surfaces instead of products that solve customer problems better.

Use when:
- Reviewing whether an AI roadmap is producing bolt-on features rather than differentiated product value.
- Deciding whether AI work should live in a central team, product team, or shared platform pattern.

Details:
- The talk frames "AI leading your products, your go to market, your strategy" as a formerly useful signal that no longer differentiates once every company says the same thing. 01:27-02:03
- Chat UIs, content UIs, multimodal demos, RAG, copilots, and agents are valid ways to learn model boundaries, but they are technology-learning moves rather than sufficient product strategy. 02:08-03:50
- Freeplay observed enterprise teams creating centralized AI strategies that run beside core product strategy; this creates separate initiatives or teams and allows non-integrated AI features to proliferate. 03:50-04:28
- The proposed operating model pairs top-level strategy with bottom-up product discovery by teams close to user problems, so AI becomes a cohesive part of the product experience instead of a separate capability showcase. 05:41-07:09
