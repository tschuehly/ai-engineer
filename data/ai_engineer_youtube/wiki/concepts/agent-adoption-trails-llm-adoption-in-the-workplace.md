# Agent Adoption Trails LLM Adoption in the Workplace

Summary: Workplace LLM adoption can look healthy while agent adoption and satisfaction remain much weaker. Agent roadmaps should separate "LLMs work for us" from "LLM-controlled workflows are reliable enough for production."

Use when:
- Deciding whether to move from assistant or copilot workflows into agent-controlled workflows.
- Framing agent maturity metrics for a team already using LLMs broadly.

Details:
- The survey defined an AI agent as a system where an LLM controls the core decision-making or workflow. (07:48-08:08)
- 80% of respondents said LLMs were working well at work, but fewer than 20% said the same about agents. (08:08-08:16)
- Fewer than one in ten respondents said they would never use agents, so low current satisfaction did not mean low future interest. (08:16-08:29)
- The majority of production agents reported in the survey had write access, typically with a human in the loop, and some could act independently; this makes permissioning and approval design part of agent adoption, not a later concern. (08:34-08:52)
