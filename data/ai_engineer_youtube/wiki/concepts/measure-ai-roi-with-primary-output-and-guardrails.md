# Measure AI ROI With Primary Output and Guardrails

Summary: AI software-engineering ROI needs a primary engineering-output metric plus guardrails for quality, rework, risk, people, and DevOps health. PR counts, lines of code, and DORA-style activity measures can rise while effective output and maintainability fall.

Use when:
- Building an executive AI ROI dashboard for software engineering.
- Challenging claims that more PRs, commits, or AI usage automatically mean productivity gains.

Details:
- Business outcomes are the ideal ROI target, but the source argues they are too noisy for direct attribution because sales execution, macro conditions, and product strategy confound the link between AI access and revenue or retention. (08:33-09:21)
- Engineering outcomes are a clearer intermediate signal, but the measurement should go beyond AI usage and avoid weaponized metrics by using a balanced set of measures. (09:21-10:23)
- The proposed framework uses a primary engineering-output metric based on an expert-panel-style model rather than lines of code, PR counts, or DORA, with guardrails for rework/refactoring, quality/technical risk, and people/DevOps. (12:00-12:51)
- A case study showed PRs increasing 14% after AI adoption, while code quality dropped 9%, rework rose 2.5x, and effective output did not meaningfully increase; PR-only ROI would have falsely looked positive. (12:57-15:28)
