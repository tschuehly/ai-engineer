# Target High-Value AI Verticals as Capability Matures

Summary: As general AI capability becomes commoditized, the highest-leverage deployment frontier shifts from horizontal tooling toward specific high-value verticals, with code-adjacent domains like finance and healthcare treated as the next areas "closest to takeoff."

Use when:
- Choosing where to apply AI engineering effort beyond generic developer tooling.
- Evaluating which industry to build a vertical AI product or practice for.
- Reading the field's direction: horizontal-track saturation versus vertical specialization.

Details:
- The framing is an explicit bet on verticals over horizontals: 2025-era horizontal tracks (design engineering, product management) are still relevant but broad, while 2026 emphasis moves to verticals where AI is deployed in high-value workflows ([6 Things to Know about AIE World's Fair 2026](../sources/20260621_0S8xe9ftGTM.md), 12:05-12:40).
- The named priority verticals are deployed engineering (forward-deployed engineering / FDE), agentic commerce, healthcare, finance, and go-to-market (GTM) (12:30-13:00).
- Finance is treated as the lead vertical and "the most likely and closest for takeoff" after coding, followed by healthcare and law — i.e., code is the first domain to mature, and adjacent high-stakes knowledge work follows (12:40-13:20).
- This complements the bottom-up view that vertical AI wins come from last-mile domain context and measurable workflow outcomes, not from chasing raw model capability.
