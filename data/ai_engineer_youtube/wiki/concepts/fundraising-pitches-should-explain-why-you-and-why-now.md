# Fundraising Pitches Should Explain Why You and Why Now

Summary: Early AI-startup fundraising should foreground the founder's unique market, customer, product, or go-to-market insight, not only the technical implementation.

Use when:
- Preparing an early pitch for an AI product before revenue or a finished product exists.
- Checking whether a technical founder's story explains why this team can win now.

Details:
- Pre-seed and seed investors can back companies before revenue or even before product because they are often underwriting the founder, team, recruiting ability, and vision rather than a stable current product. 01:00-02:42
- The talk warns that AI engineers often overfocus on technology in pitches; investors still care about technical strength, but early pitches need to explain "why you" and "why now." 14:07-14:24
- A strong pitch grounds the problem, then explains the founder's unique insight about the market, go-to-market, product, or customer segment. 14:31-15:23
- For vertical AI applications, a generic thesis such as voice AI automating phone-heavy industries is too broad; the founder should explain why a specific vertical has workflow, data, or adoption characteristics that make it the right wedge. 16:02-16:48
