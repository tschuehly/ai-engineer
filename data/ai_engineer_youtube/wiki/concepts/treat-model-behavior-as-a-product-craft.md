# Treat model behavior as a product craft

Summary: Model behavior work is a product discipline that translates principles into requirements, prompts, evals, model choices, context-window design, parameters, and shipped personality.

Use when:
- Building AI-native products where response style, autonomy, and personality are part of the user experience.
- Assigning ownership for prompts, evals, model selection, and assistant behavior across product, engineering, design, operations, and domain teams.

Details:
- The Browser Company defines model behavior as the function that defines, evaluates, and ships desired model behavior, turning product principles into requirements, prompts, evals, and the behavior or personality of an LLM product. (09:32-09:57)
- Behavior design covers the product experience, style, tone, and response shape; measurement and training data clarify requirements through evals; model steering includes prompting, model selection, context-window contents, parameters, and product implementation. (09:57-10:25)
- The process is iterative: build, refine, create evals, ship, collect internal or external feedback, and feed it back into the product-building loop. (10:25-10:43)
- A strategy and operations teammate used prompt tools to rewrite product prompts, unlock new product quality, and help form a dedicated model-behavior team, suggesting that strong contributors to behavior design may come from outside engineering. (11:47-12:47)
