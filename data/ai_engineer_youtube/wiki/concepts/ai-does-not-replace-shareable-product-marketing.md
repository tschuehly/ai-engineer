# AI Does Not Replace Shareable Product Marketing

Summary: AI can generate marketing content, but product attention still depends on ideas that people feel compelled to share. Feature announcements, ads, and generic content are weak top-of-funnel work when they do not interrupt attention or create a social reason to pass the product along.

Use when:
- Evaluating whether an AI-assisted product launch has a real attention strategy or only generated content.
- Deciding whether a product team is avoiding the uncomfortable craft of marketing by assuming a good product will sell itself.

Details:
- The source frames marketing as the problem of getting someone who does not care about the product to stop, pay attention, and consider what is being offered. (02:39-03:05)
- "Good products sell themselves" is rejected as an excuse; even strong products still need deliberate attention work. (03:20-03:56)
- Useful top-of-funnel attempts should have a plausible chance of being shared because they are surprising, funny, deep, or emotionally resonant; ordinary feature posts and influencer-style announcements usually fail that bar. (04:00-05:54)
- Raad says AI brainstorming tends to produce corny ideas and has not helped him generate genuinely cool product marketing; he treats coolness as a human skill teams must practice. (06:15-06:37)
