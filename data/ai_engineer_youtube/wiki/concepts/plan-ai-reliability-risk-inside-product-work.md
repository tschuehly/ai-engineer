# Plan AI Reliability Risk Inside Product Work

Summary: AI reliability risk should be part of ordinary product planning rather than quarantined in isolated features or teams. Product teams need evaluation, testing, prototyping, and fast failure loops to decide whether an AI capability works reliably enough to create customer value.

Use when:
- Deciding whether a proposed AI feature is ready for roadmap commitment.
- Setting product-process requirements around evals, testing, and risk for generative AI features.

Details:
- The talk names reliability as a new AI product risk: the team must ask whether the feature can work reliably enough to drive customer value. 04:32-04:52
- Quarantining AI into product corners may feel risk-reducing, but the speakers argue that teams should integrate AI risk into planning and build systems for evaluation and testing. 05:41-06:16
- Starting with the customer problem reduces technology-first failure modes such as building chatbots or document summarization only because they visibly demonstrate AI capability. 04:53-05:26, 06:16-06:27
- Bottom-up product discovery should be allowed to experiment, prototype, and fail fast under a top-level strategy so the organization learns where AI reliably improves the product. 06:27-07:09
