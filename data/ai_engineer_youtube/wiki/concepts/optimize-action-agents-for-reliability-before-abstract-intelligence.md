# Optimize Action Agents For Reliability Before Abstract Intelligence

Summary: For many business workflows, action reliability matters more than higher abstract benchmark intelligence. Agents that book, file, sort, or fill forms need production-grade success rates because failures create cleanup work and lost trust.

Use when:
- Prioritizing agent product work between broader reasoning ability and reliable execution of specific tasks.
- Explaining why "smart enough" models may still fail commercial workflows.

Details:
- Featherless AI argues that failed AI projects often fail on reliability rather than lack of scale, comparing unreliable agents to consumer apps that would be unusable if they completed orders only part of the time. (08:00-09:05)
- The pitch says companies want models reliable enough for ordinary actions such as booking airline tickets, sorting email, filing taxes, and handling invoices, not only PhD-level math. (09:05-09:25)
- The proposed target is narrow action reliability: an Action R1 agent is framed as better for filling forms reliably than for broad PhD-level reasoning. (09:34-09:53)
- The talk compresses the product lesson as "reliability is revenue": reliable boring workflows unlock e-commerce and B2B sales use cases. (10:03-10:24)
