# Specialist Expert Systems Bundle Capabilities, APIs, and Instructions

Summary: Large assistant systems can scale by packaging task-specific capabilities, APIs, and instructions into specialist expert systems that a higher-level agent can orchestrate.

Use when:
- Designing a broad assistant that must span many domains, devices, partner services, and user intents.
- Deciding whether to build one monolithic tool surface or multiple specialist capability bundles.

Details:
- Alexa Plus is described as using hundreds of specialized expert systems, where each expert bundles capabilities, APIs, and instructions to accomplish a specific task. (03:52-04:07)
- Those experts orchestrate across tens of thousands of partner services and devices, which frames large assistant reliability as service orchestration as much as model prompting. (04:07-04:19)
- The talk predicts a future of specialized agents with unique capabilities working together, rather than one universal agent directly owning every integration. (04:21-04:32)
