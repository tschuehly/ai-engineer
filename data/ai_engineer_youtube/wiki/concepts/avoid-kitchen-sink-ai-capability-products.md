# Avoid Kitchen-Sink AI Capability Products

Summary: Shipping many model capabilities is not the same as shipping a product point of view. AI products need restraint around what they should do, not only excitement about what the model can do.

Use when:
- Reviewing an AI product that includes chat, generation, agents, summaries, media, or other features without a clear primary job.
- Choosing between adding another model capability and deepening the product's core outcome.

Details:
- The talk distinguishes shipping model capabilities from shipping actual new outcomes; raw model capability is not a durable product unless it maps to a user path and product purpose. 19:05-19:30
- Product builders should know the outcome, the path users take to reach it, what blocks them, and how to show what is possible; the source argues this requires living with the product as users do. 19:20-20:13
- Huxe is used as a cautionary example: an app with chat, podcasts, news, image generation, and video generation looked exciting, but daily use and several hundred users revealed that people mostly used one thing. 23:10-24:29
- The speaker frames restraint as an innovation multiplier because focus, not feature count, lets a team do one thing well enough to delight users. 21:24-22:10
