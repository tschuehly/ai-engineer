# AI-Amplified Shipping Speed Needs Stronger Product Taste

Summary: AI can make implementation cheap enough that teams ship too many requests. Product taste becomes the constraint that decides which work should not be built, not just how quickly work can be implemented.

Use when:
- Deciding whether an AI-assisted team should ship a requested feature directly.
- Evaluating whether agentic development speed is increasing product coherence or feature sprawl.

Details:
- AI removes some of the engineering friction that previously forced teams to think before building, so teams can now say yes to too many customer requests or internal ideas. (00:46-02:15)
- The source frames competitive advantage in an AI-speed environment as building tasteful, high-quality software rather than merely matching another team's feature set. (03:14-03:49)
- Linear's workflow is to collect many customer requests, identify the underlying root problem, group related needs, and design a solution rather than shipping requests as submitted. (04:25-05:38)
