# Pivot AI Product Interfaces When Platform Defaults Shift

Summary: AI product surfaces should be revisited when commercial defaults and interoperability standards change. A custom client can remain valuable, but tools and data may need to move behind portable interfaces so users can reach them from whatever enterprise AI client becomes standard.

Use when:
- A product assumes one chat, dashboard, or custom UI will be the main way users access AI capabilities.
- Enterprise AI clients, pricing, platform defaults, or protocols such as MCP change the right distribution path.

Details:
- Redbox moved through multiple product meanings: digitizing ministerial red-box paperwork, then providing secure LLM chat for civil servants, then serving as a client for i.AI tools and data. 12:23-14:32
- The secure-chat pivot was validated when Cabinet Office launch produced thousands of users within weeks, but later platform changes made that interface strategy less durable. 14:34-14:47
- Microsoft Copilot Chat becoming free for enterprise Microsoft users changed the commercial default for many government users, while MCP created a standardized way to bring tools and data to models. 14:50-15:23
- The team pivoted from assuming Redbox would be the main secure-chat and tool-access client to investing in MCP so tools and data could reach Redbox, Copilot Chat, Anthropic, ChatGPT, or other enterprise clients. 15:24-15:54
- The product lesson is to keep evolving with the AI platform landscape; otherwise a still-useful product can become anchored to the wrong access path. 15:54-16:20
