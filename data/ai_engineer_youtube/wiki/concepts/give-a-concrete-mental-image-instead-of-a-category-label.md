# Give a Concrete Mental Image Instead of a Category Label

Summary: Make an AI product "click" by handing people a picturable image or an already-understood story ("Devin, the AI software engineer"; "a smoke alarm for AI behavior") instead of an abstract category ("agent observability platform"); the image is the front door into the conversation, and the precise technical definition comes later to close the sale.

Use when:
- Naming or describing an AI product in the first sentence of a pitch, tagline, or homepage headline.
- A description is technically accurate but leaves people unable to picture what the product actually does.
- You catch yourself opening with a category ("we're an agentic X platform for enterprise Y").

Details:
- The bar is the **17-year-old test**: "Could a 17-year-old understand what you do? If not, you're probably going to lose the room" (02:42-02:48).
- **Tie the product to a viral story people already understand.** Example: the McDonald's AI drive-thru clips (AI putting bacon on ice cream) — everyone instantly gets "AI doing something stupid in public." So instead of "we are an agent observability platform," say: "If McDonald's had used us, that drive-thru never would have made it to TikTok. We catch when AI agents go off script and give teams a chance to correct them before it becomes a PR nightmare." Now it clicks because the value is instantly visible (02:48-03:28).
- **Ban words no one can picture; give a mental image instead.** "Devin, the AI software engineer" — picturable instantly. "We're a smoke alarm for AI behavior" — also clear. These "are not perfect technical definitions, and that is fine. At this stage, they don't have to be" (03:34-03:55).
- The image is a **front door**, not the full spec: "They are the front doors into the rest of the conversation. You can give the technical version later to close the sale. Just don't make it the first thing people have to understand" (03:55-04:05).
- This is the "make it click" middle beat of the [wound → click → transformation](explain-ai-products-as-a-wound-click-transformation-story.md) pitch structure; it also sharpens why [kitchen-sink capability descriptions](avoid-kitchen-sink-ai-capability-products.md) fail — a list of capabilities gives no image to hold onto.
