# AI Engineering Practice Is Heterogeneous and Fast Moving

Summary: AI engineering work spans many job titles, use cases, model providers, and update rhythms. Teams should expect model, prompt, and workflow choices to change frequently rather than assuming a stable single-stack practice.

Use when:
- Planning an AI engineering platform or enablement program for a mixed engineering audience.
- Explaining why AI systems need model, prompt, and evaluation workflows that tolerate frequent change.

Details:
- In Amplify's 500-respondent survey, the largest group identified as engineers, but many people doing AI engineering did not have the formal title "AI engineer." (01:30-02:36)
- Many experienced software engineers were still new to AI: among software engineers with 10+ years of software experience, nearly half had worked with AI for three years or less, and one in ten started in the previous year. (02:52-03:14)
- LLM usage was not concentrated in a single workflow: more than half of respondents used LLMs for both internal and external use cases, 94% of LLM users used them for at least two use cases, and 82% used them for at least three. (03:21-04:05)
- The talk reports frequent change pressure: more than half updated models at least monthly, 17% updated weekly, 70% updated prompts at least monthly, and one in ten updated prompts daily. (05:20-05:49)
