# Run Named-Customer Feedback Loops Daily

Summary: Fast product iteration depends on working with specific named users, showing them concrete artifacts, collecting high-bandwidth feedback, and shipping improvements quickly enough that the loop stays alive.

Use when:
- Designing an early customer-development loop for an AI product or developer platform.
- Checking whether a team has real users and feedback channels before locking product decisions.

Details:
- Before locking major product decisions, the Stripe pattern was to ensure real users could see something, give feedback, and receive an improvement in less than a day when possible. 04:04-04:53
- Personas and user studies are not enough; the loop needs real people with names, emails, and current workflows the team understands directly. 05:10-05:58
- Writing a PRFAQ, launch post, press release, or rough API outline before implementation creates a concrete artifact that users can critique before the team invests in build-out. 06:13-07:06
- High-bandwidth feedback can come from shadowing users, shared Slack or Discord channels, texting relationships, and API-response monitoring with a small set of early customers. 09:04-10:43
