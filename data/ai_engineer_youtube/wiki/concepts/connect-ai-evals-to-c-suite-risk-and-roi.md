# Connect AI Evals to C-Suite Risk and ROI

Summary: Enterprise AI evaluation becomes fundable when it quantifies business risk, revenue, cost, governance, compliance, or brand exposure, not only model quality. Eval programs should translate technical behavior into the decision surfaces used by CEOs, CFOs, CISOs, CIOs, and CTOs.

Use when:
- Selling or prioritizing an eval program inside an enterprise.
- Turning model, agent, or monitoring metrics into budget, risk, or governance decisions.

Details:
- Dickerson frames AI/ML monitoring and evaluation as two sides of measurement: observability cannot work without measurable signals, and evaluation supplies the measurement layer. 01:16-01:31
- Earlier ML monitoring often stayed inside the CIO or data-science organization because model outputs were embedded in larger opaque systems and were weakly connected to downstream business KPIs. 03:40-05:04
- GenAI production use in 2024 made ROI, governance, risk, compliance, and brand optics visible to business stakeholders; quantitative risk estimates require evaluation. 09:39-10:19
- For enterprise buying and prioritization, an eval product or program should attach to downstream KPIs such as risk mitigation, revenue gains, reduced losses, budget allocation, and board reporting. 12:08-13:22
