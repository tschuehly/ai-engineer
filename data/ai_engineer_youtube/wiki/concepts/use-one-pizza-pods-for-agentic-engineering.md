# Use One-Pizza Pods for Agentic Engineering

Summary: Enterprise AI coding adoption can require smaller, broader teams rather than the classic two-pizza Agile squad. One-pizza pods of three to five people can consolidate QA, front-end, back-end, and product responsibilities into product-builder roles that orchestrate agents with full-stack and architecture fluency.

Use when:
- Designing team structures for AI-native software development.
- Explaining why role definitions must change when agents take on more execution work.

Details:
- The talk argues that eight-to-ten-person teams, two-week sprints, and Agile operating-model defaults can limit the value enterprises get from AI. (06:37-07:10)
- Top performers in the survey were described as six times more likely to have AI-native roles, including smaller pods with different skill sets and new roles. (08:58-09:16)
- AI-native roles move from two-pizza teams toward one-pizza pods of three to five people, replacing separate QA, front-end, and back-end roles with consolidated product builders. (09:55-10:14)
- Product builders are described as managing and orchestrating agents while retaining full-stack fluency and a better understanding of the full codebase architecture. (10:08-10:19)
- A client experiment used smaller pods and consolidated roles to create more pods with the same number of people while maintaining or improving the quality of generated code. (14:21-15:06)
