# Hire for AI fluency and agent orchestration ability

Summary: Agent-native organizations should evaluate whether candidates can guide, supervise, and improve agents in their domain. AI fluency becomes a role requirement when each employee is expected to manage agent counterparts for part of their work.

Use when:
- Updating hiring criteria for teams that expect employees to work through agents.
- Designing onboarding for roles that need role-specific agents, workflows, or agent-maintained context.

Details:
- The talk argues that agent-native companies need people who can directly guide agents to the task at hand and apply their domain expertise through those agents.
- New roles and titles may combine domain expertise with AI know-how, such as agent manager or AI customer lead.
- Interview processes should probe whether candidates use AI, can learn agent-guidance practices, and can bring in or develop teams that use agents effectively.
- Onboarding may need to include installing or configuring the agentic tools and systems that make a new hire productive, including time spent setting up agents for the role.
