# Shape AI Teams Around the Bottleneck

Summary: AI team composition should follow the company's actual constraint, not a generic desire for "AI talent." The missing capability may be domain knowledge, product integration, data, model serving, customer empathy, ROI measurement, reliability, observability, or specialist model work.

Use when:
- Deciding whether to hire researchers, AI engineers, product/domain experts, infrastructure engineers, or customer-facing generalists.
- Diagnosing why an AI mandate is not translating into shipped product or business value.

Details:
- Company type affects the skill gap: technology companies may need domain/data expertise, tech-enabled companies may need technology capability, and verticalized companies need the two sides to reinforce each other, 01:13-02:40.
- AI delivery work spans use-case definition, product integration, ROI, data, workflow testing, interfaces, selling, and customer adoption; no single role should be assumed to own all of it, 05:13-05:52.
- Before hiring, identify whether the blocker is feature shipping, acquisition, retention, monetization, scalability, reliability, or observability, 06:10-06:42.
- Reflexively hiring top AI researchers is often the wrong first move unless the product itself is frontier model work or requires specialty model research, 03:53-04:33.
