# Counterbalance output incentives with independent scoping and QA

Summary: Output-based engineering incentives need counterweights because they can reward inflated estimates, rushed delivery, or adversarial collaboration. Separate scoping, acceptance, QA, customer feedback, and selective hiring are part of the compensation system, not optional process overhead.

Use when:
- Adding performance pay, bounties, or story-point compensation to engineering work.
- Evaluating whether an AI-accelerated team is optimizing for throughput at the expense of quality.
- Designing review gates around agent-assisted implementation work.

Details:
- The talk names three direct risks of story-point compensation: engineers may inflate story points, rush work until quality drops, or behave with sharp elbows toward teammates. (12:00-12:49)
- Tenex reduces estimate inflation by having strategists scope the work and by reviewing the scoping before engineering execution. (13:18-13:24)
- Tenex reduces quality risk through multiple rounds of internal QA plus client approval for every delivered ticket; strategist compensation is tied to NRR or customer happiness, creating a counterbalance to pure engineering throughput. (12:52-13:15)
- The talk argues hiring becomes more important with AI because AI amplifies existing attributes: strong engineers become more effective, while weak engineers can become sloppier. (13:35-14:08)
