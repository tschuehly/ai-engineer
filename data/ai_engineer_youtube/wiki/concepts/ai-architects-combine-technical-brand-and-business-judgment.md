# AI Architects Combine Technical, Brand, and Business Judgment

Summary: Customer-facing agents create a role that blends technical capability sense, brand and experience taste, and business-outcome ownership. The role is less about pretraining models and more about shaping what the agent can do, how it comes across, and what customer or business goal it advances.

Use when:
- Defining ownership for a branded customer-facing agent.
- Deciding whether CX, support, product, design, or engineering should steer agent behavior.

Details:
- Bavor compares the AI architect to the early web master: someone responsible for a company's new digital storefront across technology, look, feel, and user experience. (02:38-03:13)
- The role has three hats: understand agent capabilities, make brand and persona decisions such as voice, values, tone, sound, and connection, and drive business outcomes through customer engagement. (03:36-05:03)
- The role can emerge from customer experience, support, care, retail, engineering, or other teams, but people close to customers can be especially effective when they become hands-on enough with agent technology. (05:12-06:10)
- The agent should manifest both the company's functional capabilities and its brand, so agent design includes choices about persona, humor, empathy, and judgment rather than only tool access. (03:56-04:48)
