# Voice Generation Safety Needs Moderation, Consent, and Provenance

Summary: Voice-generation platforms need controls around what synthetic voices can say, who is allowed to clone a voice, and how generated speech can be traced back after abuse. Moderation, voice-capture consent checks, and watermarking make fraud response part of the voice-agent platform rather than an afterthought.

Use when:
- Shipping synthetic voice, voice cloning, or public voice-library features.
- Designing abuse controls for generated audio in conversational products.

Details:
- The workshop names fraud and scam use as a real safety concern for voice tools and points to platform safety tooling developed alongside product features. 49:06-49:33
- Published voices can specify terms they should not say, and live moderation can block a voice from generating specific terms or sentences. 49:33-50:05
- Generated speech is watermarked so abuse investigations can trace a piece of generated audio back to the account that created it. 50:04-50:48
- Professional voice cloning uses a random sentence readout as a voice-capture verification step to check that the user has permission to clone the voice. 51:00-51:34
