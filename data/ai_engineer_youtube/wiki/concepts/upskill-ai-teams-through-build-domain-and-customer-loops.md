# Upskill AI Teams Through Build, Domain, and Customer Loops

Summary: AI team upskilling should be operational, not just educational: people need to build functional prototypes, deepen domain expertise, write evaluations, and face customers so feedback loops shorten.

Use when:
- Designing an enablement program for an existing team under an AI mandate.
- Moving domain experts and engineers from static handoffs into shared AI-product iteration.

Details:
- Linkov recommends three upskilling directions: learn to build, become a domain expert, and become human-facing, 12:46-13:01.
- Functional prototypes should replace static product requirements where possible because they expose details and edge cases faster than requirement debates, 13:01-13:21.
- Domain experts should define use cases and write evaluations, not only provide passive input or feedback, 13:21-13:30.
- Engineers should join customer calls so product feedback reaches builders directly; refusal to talk to customers is treated as a learning opportunity, 13:32-13:43.
- A weekly 30-minute learning cadence can keep the team current on company priorities and AI practice; the stated risk is that not learning is more expensive than the time spent, 13:46-14:06.
