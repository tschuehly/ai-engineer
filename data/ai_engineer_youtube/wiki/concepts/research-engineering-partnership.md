# Research Engineering Partnership

Summary: Frontier AI progress depends on research ideas and engineering systems maturing together. A strong idea cannot live without the infrastructure, kernels, orchestration, and interfaces that make it executable, while engineering effort needs research direction to matter.

Use when:
- Explaining why AI engineering can contribute at the same level as research.
- Designing organizations or projects where model research, systems engineering, and product engineering must coordinate.

Details:
- Brockman frames AlexNet as a research-engineering combination: fast GPU convolution kernels became transformative when paired with the idea to apply them to ImageNet. 16:41-17:30
- At current scale, the engineering side is no longer only kernels; it includes systems that can scale to 100,000 GPUs and orchestrate complex reinforcement-learning workloads. 17:38-17:55
- The interface between research and engineering is a recurring coordination problem: engineers prefer stable abstractions, while researchers often need to understand system behavior behind the interface to make progress. 18:44-19:24
- The partnership is never permanently solved; teams solve the current level of coordination and then encounter a harder version at the next sophistication level. 18:44-18:55
