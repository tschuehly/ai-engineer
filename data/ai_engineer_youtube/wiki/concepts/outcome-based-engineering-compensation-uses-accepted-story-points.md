# Outcome-based engineering compensation uses accepted story points

Summary: AI-accelerated engineering teams can align compensation with delivered output by paying a base plus a per-story-point amount after accepted tickets. This turns story points into an operational charge and pay unit rather than only an estimation ritual.

Use when:
- Designing compensation for AI-augmented engineering teams or agencies.
- Comparing hourly, project, salary, equity, and output-based incentives.
- Choosing a billable unit for custom AI implementation work.

Details:
- Tenex's model charges clients for the story points delivered and pays engineers based on completed story points, tying client spend and engineer upside to accepted output rather than hours worked. (01:12-01:39)
- The implementation workflow starts with roadmapping, then product requirements are distilled by strategists, architecture design documents are produced by engineers, and tickets are assigned story points before execution. (07:36-09:06)
- Engineers receive a flat base, and each quarter Tenex rounds up compensation based on story points completed after ticket acceptance. (09:10-09:22)
- The model is presented as a response to AI-driven productivity changes: when agent tooling can greatly increase output, paying only for time or headcount can weaken the incentive to adopt faster workflows. (07:20-07:33, 14:10-14:26)
