# Agentic Coding Collapses Coordination Tax for Small Valuable Changes

Summary: Agentic coding can turn some small but valuable work from backlog negotiation into direct implementation. The core value is not only faster typing, but removing coordination delays when the problem owner can safely produce or prototype the change.

Use when:
- Evaluating AI coding use cases outside traditional developer tickets.
- Deciding whether a support, product, design, or leadership user should prototype a bounded feature.
- Explaining why AI coding may change organizational workflow even when code generation quality is imperfect.

Details:
- Kim says one reason to use AI coding is that tedious or small tasks become cheap enough to fix on the spot instead of entering backlog grooming and coordination cycles. (14:27-15:01)
- He frames LLMs as high-bandwidth intermediation vehicles that can coordinate across specialties through artifacts such as Markdown files, helping align shared goals and understanding. (15:39-16:01)
- The Fidelity example describes a leader spending five days vibe-coding a long-requested internal application against read-only Neo4j data after the team had repeatedly estimated it at months plus front-end hiring. (19:19-19:40)
- The follow-up caveat is ownership: after the prototype reached production, maintainership still mattered, and a junior engineer took on the work while demand for the app increased. (19:53-20:16)
