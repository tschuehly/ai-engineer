# Hire Humans for Context, Verification, and Accountability

Summary: AI agents can help act over large contexts, but human roles still matter because organizations need people who hold context, verify whether context and execution are correct, and remain accountable for the systems being built.

Use when:
- Evaluating claims that large-context agents remove the need for human team members.
- Defining why a role should exist in an AI-heavy organization.

Details:
- Linkov frames hiring around two functions: holding context and acting on context, 14:07-14:18.
- Large-context agents may handle some of this work, but expertise is still required to verify that the supplied context and execution are correct, 14:24-14:35.
- Expertise depends on accumulated context, so replacing context-holding humans can also weaken the verification layer, 14:35-14:39.
- Humans remain accountable for built systems because organizations cannot hold a machine accountable, 14:39-14:47.
- Hiring decisions should be based on first-principles team composition and role needs rather than trends such as blanket avoidance of junior engineers, 15:07-15:54.
