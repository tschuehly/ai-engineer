# Build AI product iteration tools into the product context

Summary: AI-product teams should place prompt, context, model, tool, and parameter iteration inside the same product context where real work happens, so non-engineers can test behavior with realistic personal and workflow context.

Use when:
- Designing internal tooling for an AI-native product team.
- Deciding whether prompt and model-behavior iteration should happen in isolated developer tools or in the product itself.

Details:
- A dev-only prompt editor limited iteration to engineers, slowed changes, and lacked the personal context needed to judge whether an AI product felt useful. (04:45-05:11)
- Dia embedded internal iteration tools into the product the company used daily, including prompts, tools, context, models, and parameters. (05:11-05:25)
- Moving tools into the product reportedly increased ideation, iteration, and refinement speed while letting the CEO, new hires, PMs, designers, support, strategy, and operations create or refine AI behaviors with their full context. (05:25-06:26)
- The same pattern applied beyond prompts: the team built tools for memory-knowledge-graph optimization and tested many computer-use strategies before adding one to the product. (05:44-06:05)
