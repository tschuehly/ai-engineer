# AI Product Management Balances Viability, Feasibility, Desirability, and AI Uncertainty

Summary: AI product management extends ordinary viability, feasibility, and desirability judgment with AI-specific uncertainty. Someone on the team needs enough AI expertise to decide whether the model behavior is possible, measurable, safe, useful, and worth building.

Use when:
- Deciding who should own product direction for an AI feature or product.
- Translating AI engineering uncertainty into product scope, guardrails, and user workflow choices.

Details:
- Traditional product management asks whether a product is viable for the business, feasible for the team, and desirable to users; AI adds uncertainty around experimentation cost, higher failure probability, model performance monitoring, probabilistic behavior, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop design. 02:25-03:44
- The "AI product manager" can be a role or a mindset, but the responsibility is concrete: one person or small group must connect AI/data proficiency, evaluation, product pathfinding, and user impact rather than splitting them across a committee. 03:47-04:53
- Technical AI engineers are plausible owners for this mindset because understanding data, evaluation, and probabilistic model behavior is now part of product direction, not only implementation detail. 03:47-04:28
