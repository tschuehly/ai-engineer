# Ship Continuously Viable Product Shapes

Summary: Product iteration should preserve a usable end-to-end path at every stage instead of assembling disconnected parts that only become valuable at the end.

Use when:
- Planning an AI-assisted prototype or product slice.
- Deciding whether a feature plan gives users something real enough to test before the full system exists.

Details:
- Auchenberg contrasts building wheels, chassis, and engine separately with building a skateboard, scooter, bike, and then car; the second path keeps the user able to move from A to B at every stage. 02:21-03:16
- A continuously viable product shape produces feedback after each incremental change, avoids building in a vacuum, and is more valuable than a solution that only becomes usable at the end. 03:17-03:49
- AI-assisted prototyping strengthens this pattern because product leaders and engineers can now assemble runnable product shapes quickly instead of stopping at paper prototypes or abstract debates. 08:06-08:54
