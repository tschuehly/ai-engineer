# Benchmark AI Engineering Practices by Usage Pattern

Summary: AI adoption measurement should inspect how teams use AI, not only whether they have access or spend tokens. Repository and telemetry traces can classify work from no AI use through personal use, shared team rules, task-level autonomy, and broader agentic orchestration.

Use when:
- Comparing AI adoption maturity across teams with similar tool access.
- Designing dashboards that separate AI-use quality from license rollout or token spend.

Details:
- The source proposes scanning codebases and git history for AI "fingerprints" that show what percentage of active engineering work uses each AI pattern. (06:17-06:53)
- The described maturity levels move from no AI use, to personal unversioned prompts, to shared team prompts and rules, to AI doing specific tasks, to agentic orchestration of whole processes. (06:53-07:32)
- In one case study, two business units had equal AI licenses, spend, and tools but showed very different adoption and usage rates, so leaders needed to understand how engineers used AI rather than only whether they had access. (07:35-08:27)
