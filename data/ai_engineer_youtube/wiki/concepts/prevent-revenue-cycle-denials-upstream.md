# Prevent Revenue Cycle Denials Upstream

Summary: Revenue cycle AI should use end-to-end workflow history to prevent avoidable denials before submission, not only automate appeals after failure. Late denial outcomes can be connected back to missing data, prior authorization issues, policy changes, and registration or documentation errors.

Use when:
- Designing operational AI that can see both upstream process data and downstream business outcomes.
- Deciding whether an AI workflow should optimize remediation or prevent the error earlier.

Details:
- The source argues many denials do not need a smarter appeal system; they need earlier prevention of technical errors such as registration mistakes or missing data, 09:19-09:41.
- Ensemble's end-to-end RCM position lets it connect longitudinal data from prior authorization requests through final denials and intervene before the error occurs, 09:46-11:17.
- Prior authorization is hard because payer requirements are unclear, portals must be checked, and policies can change even after a query says authorization is not required, 10:10-10:48.
- AI can flag early procedure or documentation patterns that historically lead to denial, such as a missing procedure in an original document, 11:13-11:35.
